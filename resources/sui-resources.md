# Sui Developer Resources 2025-2026

A curated collection of research papers, documentation, videos, and community hubs for Sui Move developers and security researchers.

- [Sui Move Ontology Paper (2025)](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/15/2/689): Pattern-Oriented research for secure Move development.
